Aurelia in figures

    About & methodology

    The Aurelia National Statistics Office (ANSO) is the country’s independent statistics authority. It publishes the figures on this site under the Statistics Act of the Republic of Aurelia.

    How the figures are made

    • Population: mid-year estimates from the 2025 Census, updated with births, deaths and migration registers.
    • Economy: national accounts compiled to the Aurelian System of National Accounts; GDP at current prices, growth in volume terms.
    • Prices: the Consumer Price Index tracks a basket of 412 goods and services priced monthly in all six provinces.
    • Labour: the quarterly Labour Force Survey interviews 12,400 households; unemployment follows the ILO definition.
    • Health: life tables from the Mortality Register; hospital figures from the annual Health Survey of all 86 hospitals.
    • Education: enrolment from the school census each September; literacy from the 2025 Census.
